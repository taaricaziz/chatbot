The mandate

All insurance business relating to any public property, or to any risk or liability appertaining to any public property, shall be placed with the National Insurance Company Limited only. Insurance Ordinance, 2000 · Section 166(3)

One insurer, so the state's risk is pooled once.

The mandate is not a privilege; it is a duty. Because every ministry, authority and corporation places its cover here, NICL carries a single, national view of public risk and builds reserves against it.

That pool lets the company retain more risk on its own balance sheet and place the rest with reinsurers on the strength of the whole portfolio rather than one department's. It also lets NICL advise government bodies on risk management before a loss, not after.

When something goes wrong

A claim moves in four steps. The first one is yours.

Notify the office that issued the policy as soon as the loss is known. Everything after that is NICL's job to drive, and the survey is independent by law.

  1. Notify

    Call the UAN or write to the issuing office within 48 hours with the policy number, the date and a plain description of what happened.

  2. Secure and record

    Prevent further loss, keep damaged property for inspection, and lodge an FIR where theft, riot or malicious damage is involved.

  3. Independent survey

    NICL appoints a surveyor licensed by the SECP. The surveyor's report, not the company's opinion, establishes the loss.

  4. Settlement

    Once documents are complete the claim is admitted and paid to the insured's designated account.
