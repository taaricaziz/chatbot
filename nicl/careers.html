How selection works

  1. Apply against the advertisement with a CV and the documents it lists. Applications sent for a position that is not advertised are not kept on file.
  2. Shortlisting is on the published criteria: qualification, relevant experience and, for professional grades, membership of the Pakistan Insurance Institute or the Chartered Insurance Institute.
  3. Written test and interview before a selection board that includes an independent member.
  4. Offers follow Government of Pakistan pay scales for public-sector companies, with the quota for provinces and regions applied as prescribed.

What we look for

Judgement about risk, and the honesty to say when a risk should not be written. NICL's business is the state's property; the people who underwrite it are trusted accordingly.
