Classes of cover

Twelve classes of general insurance.

Every policy is written on NICL's own paper, reinsured on the international market, and issued by the office nearest the insured. Sums insured, deductibles and endorsements are agreed at proposal.

FIR · Fire & Allied Perils

Fire & Allied Perils

The standard fire policy insures buildings, plant, machinery, furniture and stock against fire and lightning. Allied perils are added by endorsement so that a department pays only for the exposures its site actually has.

What the policy responds to

  • Fire and lightning
  • Explosion
  • Riot, strike and malicious damage
  • Atmospheric disturbance: storm, flood, cyclone
  • Earthquake, fire and shock
  • Impact by vehicles and aircraft
  • Burglary and housebreaking (extension)
  • Loss of rent and business interruption (extension)

Sum insured on reinstatement value. Schedules list each location separately, so a departmental estate of many buildings sits under one policy with one renewal date.

Arrange this cover

Public bodies write to the office for their region with a schedule of the assets and their values. A proposal form and a site survey follow where the class requires one.

MCA · Marine Cargo

Marine Cargo

Cargo cover follows the goods rather than the vessel: from the moment they leave the supplier's warehouse until they reach the consignee's, by sea, air, rail or road, including transhipment.

What the policy responds to

  • Institute Cargo Clauses (A), (B) or (C)
  • War and strikes clauses
  • Inland transit within Pakistan
  • Project cargo and heavy lifts
  • Open cover for regular government imports
  • Duty and increased-value endorsements

Public-sector imports are declared under an open cover, so a letter of credit can be opened without waiting for a fresh policy each time.

MHU · Marine Hull

Marine Hull

Hull cover insures the vessel itself, its machinery and equipment, and the liabilities that follow from operating it. NICL writes fleets belonging to ports, shipping corporations and provincial authorities.

What the policy responds to

  • Hull and machinery, all risks
  • Increased value and disbursements
  • Collision liability
  • Port risks for laid-up vessels
  • Dredgers, tugs, pilot boats and barges
  • Builders' risks for vessels under construction

Valued policies, surveyed at inception. Claims are adjusted on the Institute Time Clauses that international reinsurers recognise.

Arrange this cover

AVH · Aviation Hull

Aviation Hull

Aircraft belonging to public bodies, their spares and the liabilities that attach to flying them are insured here and placed with specialist aviation reinsurers in London and the region.

What the policy responds to

  • Aircraft hull, all risks, on the ground and in flight
  • Hull war risks
  • Spares and engines
  • Passenger, third-party and cargo liability
  • Airport operators' liability
  • Loss of licence for crew (by arrangement)

Agreed-value hull cover. Fleet schedules are reviewed at every renewal so that the sum insured tracks the market, not the purchase price.

Arrange this cover

MOT · Motor

Motor

Departmental fleets, from a district office's single vehicle to a corporation's thousands, are written under fleet arrangements with one renewal date and one schedule.

What the policy responds to

  • Accidental damage to the vehicle
  • Fire and external explosion
  • Theft and burglary
  • Malicious damage
  • Riot and strike damage
  • Third-party bodily injury and property damage
  • Personal accident for the driver (extension)
  • Comprehensive, third-party-fire-and-theft, or third-party-only

Cars, jeeps, commercial vehicles, buses, motorcycles and specialised plant. The motor portfolio is written exclusively for the public sector.

ENG · Engineering

Engineering

Engineering cover follows a project through its life: all risks while it is built, then breakdown once it runs. Large national projects, including CPEC infrastructure, are insured on this class.

What the policy responds to

  • Contractors' All Risks (CAR)
  • Erection All Risks (EAR)
  • Contractors' Plant and Machinery
  • Machinery Breakdown
  • Boiler and Pressure Plant
  • Electronic Equipment and Computer All Risks
  • Deterioration of Stock
  • Advance Loss of Profits (by arrangement)

Project policies run from site handover to commissioning, with maintenance and testing periods scheduled explicitly.

CRP · Crop Loan Insurance

Crop Loan Insurance

Under the Crop Loan Insurance Scheme, farmers borrowing for seasonal inputs are covered against natural calamities. NICL has written this class since the Rabi season of 2008-09.

What the policy responds to

  • Flood, excessive rain and hail
  • Drought and frost
  • Locust attack and viral or bacterial disease
  • Loan-linked sum insured for each season
  • Kharif and Rabi seasons written separately
  • Premium subsidy administered with the lending bank for subsistence farmers

Cover attaches to the loan, so the borrower's obligation to the bank is discharged when the harvest is lost.

CRS · Credit Risk & Suretyship

Credit Risk & Suretyship

Bonds and credit cover let public bodies contract with confidence. NICL issues the guarantees that procurement rules ask of a bidder or contractor, and insures receivables against non-payment.

What the policy responds to

  • Bid bonds
  • Performance bonds
  • Advance-payment and retention-money bonds
  • Customs and excise bonds
  • Fidelity guarantee for employees handling cash
  • Credit insurance on trade receivables

Bonds are issued against counter-indemnity and, where required, collateral. Wording follows the procuring agency's standard forms.

LIA · Liability

Liability

Liability cover answers the claims of third parties: people injured on premises, products that fail, advice that goes wrong, and decisions taken by boards.

What the policy responds to

  • Public liability
  • Product liability
  • Professional indemnity
  • Directors' and officers' liability
  • Employer's liability (beyond statutory compensation)
  • Legal costs and defence expenses

Written on a claims-made or occurrence basis according to the class, with limits set per occurrence and in the aggregate.

WCA · Workers' Compensation

Workers' Compensation

Every employer owes compensation to a worker injured at work under the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923. This policy pays that obligation and can extend beyond it.

What the policy responds to

  • Death and permanent total disablement
  • Permanent partial disablement
  • Temporary disablement, weekly compensation
  • Medical expenses (extension)
  • Occupational disease (extension)
  • Employer's liability at common law (extension)

Premium follows the wage roll and the occupation, with an adjustment at expiry on the actual wages paid.

AHE · Accident & Health

Accident & Health

Group schemes for the workforce of public institutions, written for the whole staff so that no individual is underwritten out.

What the policy responds to

  • Group personal accident, 24-hour or on-duty
  • Group hospitalisation and surgical expenses
  • Maternity (extension)
  • Outpatient and pharmacy (extension)
  • Repatriation and emergency evacuation
  • Panel hospitals nationwide

Benefits are set per grade, and a scheme can be arranged for any institution with a defined roll of employees.

TRV · Travel

Travel

Cover for official delegations and for individuals travelling abroad: the medical emergency that cannot be paid for in cash, the bag that never arrives, the trip that has to be cancelled.

What the policy responds to

  • Emergency medical expenses abroad
  • Emergency evacuation and repatriation
  • Loss of passport and baggage
  • Baggage and flight delay
  • Trip cancellation and curtailment
  • Personal liability while abroad

Single-trip or annual multi-trip, with Schengen-compliant limits available on request.
