About NICL

A statutory corporation that became a company, and kept its duty.

National Insurance Company Limited is wholly owned by the Government of Pakistan and works under the administrative control of the Ministry of Commerce. It exists to insure the property of the state.

Heritage

The National Insurance Corporation was created in 1976 by the National Insurance Corporation Act, with a paid-up capital of Rs 5 million and a single purpose: to insure public property.

For a quarter of a century it did so as a statutory body. On 31 March 2000 it was incorporated as National Insurance Company Limited, an unquoted public limited company under the Companies Ordinance, 1984, through the NIC (Reorganization) Ordinance, 2000. In January 2001 the Government raised its paid-up capital to Rs 2 billion.

The change from a corporation to a company was deliberate. It put NICL on a commercial footing, subject to the Insurance Ordinance, 2000 and to the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan like every other insurer, while leaving its duty to the state intact.

Mandate and functions

Section 166(3) of the Insurance Ordinance, 2000 requires that all insurance business relating to public property be placed with NICL and no other insurer.

The company's functions follow from that sentence:

  • To insure the movable and immovable property of the Federal and Provincial Governments, local authorities and statutory corporations.
  • To build reserves sufficient to raise the company's own retention of risk, so that less of the national premium leaves the country as reinsurance.
  • To advise government departments on risk management, so that losses are prevented and not merely paid for.
  • To write motor insurance exclusively for the public sector.
Market positionThe Competition Commission of Pakistan's 2025 assessment of the insurance sector records that NICL holds the whole of the public-property segment and around 17.5% of general insurance overall. The Commission has recommended that the Federal Government review Section 166; any change is for Parliament, and until then the mandate stands as written.

Board and management

NICL is governed by a Board of Directors appointed by the Government of Pakistan under the Public Sector Companies (Corporate Governance) Rules, 2013, with a Chairman drawn from the private sector and a Chief Executive Officer appointed through open competition. The Board's audit, human-resource and risk committees meet on a published calendar.

The company's leadership pages carry the current Chairman, Chief Executive Officer, directors and heads of department. Names and portraits are maintained by the Company Secretary and change with each appointment.

Subsidiary

NICL's wholly owned subsidiary was incorporated in 1994 and holds a paid-up capital of Rs 448.20 million. It manages and lets the company's real estate, including NICL Building on Abbasi Shaheed Road and NIC House in Karachi, and the NICL buildings in Lahore and Islamabad.

Financial reports

Annual audited accounts are published each year and filed with the SECP, together with the Directors' Report and the Statement of Compliance with the corporate-governance rules. Older reports remain available.

Governance and integrity

As a public-sector company NICL procures under the Public Procurement Regulatory Authority's rules, publishes its tenders, and reports to the Ministry of Commerce and to Parliament through the Ministry. The company observes an Integrity Pact on major procurement and participates in Transparency International Pakistan's monitoring.
