Claims

Report a loss, then let the process run.

Notify the office that issued the policy as soon as the loss is known, in any form: a call, an e-mail, a letter. A late notice is the commonest reason a valid claim is delayed.

Notify within 48 hours

Call 111-642-642 from any city, or 0800-06425 toll-free, and give the policy number, the date of loss and a plain description. Written confirmation follows by e-mail to the issuing office. For theft, riot, strike or malicious damage, lodge an FIR with the police station of the area at once; the survey cannot begin without it.

Do not dispose of damaged propertyKeep everything for the surveyor to inspect, unless holding it would cause further loss or endanger people. Photograph it first in either case.

Documents, by class of cover

Tick the items off as you gather them. The list is what the surveyor will ask for; sending it complete is the single thing that most shortens a claim.

Survey and settlement

NICL appoints an independent surveyor licensed by the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan, as the Insurance Ordinance requires for any loss above the prescribed threshold. The surveyor inspects, asks for documents, and reports on the cause and quantum of loss. The company adjusts the claim on that report, applies the policy's deductible and conditions, and pays the admitted amount to the insured's designated account.

Acknowledgement
Within three working days of notice, with a claim reference
Surveyor
Appointed within seven working days of notice for property and engineering losses
Decision
Communicated in writing, with reasons, once the surveyor's report and documents are complete
Disagreement
Raised first with the claims department, then through the complaints forums
